- Women and Children Helpline 109: https://mowca.gov.bd/ (listed by the government portal) and https://www.police.gov.bd/en/hot_line_number
- Child Helpline 1098: https://msw.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/694032e235ce18e1c0563e15
- National Legal Aid Helpline 16699: https://nlaso.gov.bd/pages/notices/6922f24ddbfbab28ce0d852e

Re-check all contact information, service scope, hours, law/policy text, safeguarding pathways, privacy and retention rules immediately before production release.
